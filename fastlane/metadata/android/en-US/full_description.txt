PowerPing is a charge-threshold alarm. Plug in your phone and it rings an alarm the moment the battery reaches the level you set — unplug in time, protect battery health, and stop overnight overcharging.

<b>Features</b>

* Charge target alarm: pick a threshold between 50% and 95%. When your battery reaches it during charging, a looping alarm (following the system alarm volume) reminds you to unplug.
* One alert per charge: smart debounce — the alarm fires once per charging session, so tiny fluctuations around the threshold won't re-trigger it. Unplugging resets it for the next session.
* Zero-drain monitoring: event-driven battery tracking via system broadcasts. No polling, no wasted battery.
* Auto-resume: monitoring restarts automatically after reboot and when a charger is plugged in.
* Alarm preview: try the alert sound any time with one tap.
* Lightweight single-screen UI with live charge status.

<b>Notes</b>

* The alert follows the system <i>alarm volume</i> — if you don't hear it, check that the alarm volume isn't muted.
* On aggressive ROMs (MIUI/HyperOS, EMUI, ColorOS), please allow auto-start and background running so monitoring isn't killed.

PowerPing is free and open-source software, licensed under the GNU GPL v3. It contains no ads, no trackers and requests no unnecessary permissions.
